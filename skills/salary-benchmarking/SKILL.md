# Salary Benchmarking

## When to use
When producing salary intelligence for a profile (current-role benchmarking) or a
salary-goal gap analysis (Career Aspiration Mode).

## Method
1. Establish a baseline range for the role, experience band, and location using the
   grounding data and any market-research findings available.
2. Apply adjustments in this order:
   - Education tier (IIT/NIT premium, ~20%)
   - Skill premium (GenAI/emerging skills, ~20-30%)
   - Company tier (product company vs. services vs. startup)
3. State the candidate's estimated market range and where their current pay falls in it.
4. If a goal salary is given, compute the gap and identify the smallest set of levers
   (skill, company tier, role) that close it.

## Output format
- Market range (low–mid–high)
- Candidate's estimated position in that range
- Gap to any stated goal, in both percentage and levers required

## Rules
- Never invent a specific number for a real company's pay without it being sourced from
  research or grounding; state ranges and cite the basis (grounding vs. live search).
- Always show your adjustment factors, not just a final number.
