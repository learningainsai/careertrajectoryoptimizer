Career Trajectory Optimizer — AI career coach for Indian tech professionals
A Deep Agents system that parses your profile, researches the live Indian tech market, benchmarks your salary, and scores your risk — then, if you state a goal, builds a personalized 30-60-90 day roadmap. One orchestrator plans and delegates to eight specialist subagents; nothing is synced to a calendar without your approval.

The problem with career decisions
Indian tech professionals make career decisions with fragmented, incomplete information. Salary data is scattered across AmbitionBox, Levels.fyi, and Glassdoor. GenAI roles are growing roughly 180% year-over-year, but few people know it. Nothing correlates your actual profile — skills, tenure, education tier — against the market and against a stated goal. A generic chatbot will happily invent a plausible-sounding salary number or timeline; in a career decision, a confident wrong answer costs you months.
Why it’s built this way
Reads your whole profile
profile-analyzer parses a resume upload (PDF/DOCX/TXT) or manual entry into a structured timeline, detecting education tier and flagging job-hopping or long tenure.
Researches live, not stale
market-researcher is the only subagent with web search (LinkUp primary, Tavily fallback) — it gathers current data so every other subagent reasons from the same grounded brief.
Never invents a number
Every salary range and risk score traces back to the grounding file or the researcher’s findings — deterministic validators catch missing or out-of-range values before they reach you.
Explains your gap
career-recommender applies goal-specific logic — target salary, company, role, or GenAI transition — and states the gap and the levers that close it.
Plans its own steps
The orchestrator classifies your brief into Profile Analysis Mode or Career Aspiration Mode and delegates accordingly, running independent subagents concurrently to cut wall-clock time.
Reviewed before you see it
A dedicated critic subagent checks grounding accuracy, methodology compliance, and internal consistency across every file before the run completes.
