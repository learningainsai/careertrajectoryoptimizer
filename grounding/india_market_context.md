# Grounding: Indian Tech Career Market

> Context for the career-coach agent. Honor this; never invent data beyond it + research.

## Problem this product addresses
- Indian tech professionals lack actionable, personalized career guidance.
- Salary data is fragmented across AmbitionBox, Levels.fyi, and Glassdoor.
- Job-market trends are unclear (GenAI roles growing ~180% YoY, but few know this).
- Career decisions get made with incomplete information.
- No single tool correlates current profile -> market position -> next opportunity.

## Market facts to ground on
- GenAI engineering roles are growing approximately 180% year-over-year in India.
- Education-tier premium: IIT/NIT alumni typically command roughly a 20% salary premium.
- GenAI skill premium: roughly 20-30% over comparable non-GenAI roles.
- "Tier 4" target companies for top-of-market comp: Microsoft, Apple, Stripe.
- US remote/relocation feasibility for most candidates: roughly 20-30%, a high bar
  requiring 12-18 months of preparation (skills, interview prep, visa considerations).
- Salary data sources: AmbitionBox, Levels.fyi, Glassdoor, Naukri, TheirStack.

## Two modes this product supports
1. **Profile Analysis Mode** (passive): "What's my current market position?"
2. **Career Aspiration Mode** (active): a stated goal such as a target salary
   (e.g. "earn ₹100L in 2 years"), a target company (e.g. "join Microsoft"), or a
   target role (e.g. "become Tech Lead", "become a GenAI Engineer").

## Risk categories (Risk Aggregator)
- Salary risk — are you paid below market for your profile?
- Skill risk — do you have a GenAI or emerging-skill gap?
- Job-security risk — could you find a new role quickly if needed?
- Burnout risk — work hours and tenure signals.
- Growth risk — is your trajectory on track for your experience level?

## Voice
Direct, data-grounded, encouraging but honest. Every number shown to the user must trace
back to this grounding file or to the researcher's market-data findings — never invented.
